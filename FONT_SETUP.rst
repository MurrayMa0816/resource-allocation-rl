Plot fonts
==========

The original experiment used locally installed Chinese and Windows fonts.
Fonts with unestablished redistribution permission are excluded.
Provide your own legally installed fonts using the paths documented in
assets/fonts/CANONICAL_FONTS.json, or adapt only your local plotting
font configuration. Font substitution can change figure layout.
The saved scientific numbers and model weights are unaffected.
Third-party fonts with their own included licenses retain those licenses.
